## 🎯 Your 3-Step Launch Plan

### Step 1: Add Images (15 minutes)
- The homepage now displays "VADORA" prominently with a logo placeholder
- **Logo (optional):** Add your logo to replace the placeholder box - see QUICK-START-GUIDE.md
- Add your 6 product images to the `assets/images/` folder
- Required: hygiene products, cleaning supplies, office supplies, refuse bags, paper bags, wholesale

### Step 2: Update Contact Info (10 minutes)
- Open `CONTACT-INFO-TEMPLATE.txt` and fill it out
- Open `PLACEHOLDER-UPDATE-GUIDE.md` for exact instructions
- Replace 8 placeholders in `index.html`:
  - Business address (2 times)
  - Business email (2 times)
  - Business hours (2 times)
  - TikTok URL (2 times)

### Step 3: Test & Launch (20 minutes)
- Open `index.html` in your browser to preview
- Follow `TESTING-CHECKLIST.md`
- Test WhatsApp buttons on your phone
- Upload to your web hosting
