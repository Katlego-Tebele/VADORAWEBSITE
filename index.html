About VADORA Supply Solutions

VADORA Supply Solutions (Pty) Ltd is a supplier of essential cleaning, hygiene, packaging and office consumable products. We focus on providing reliable supply and practical solutions for businesses across various industries.

Our product range includes cleaning products, hygiene essentials, refuse bags, paper bags, office consumables, and wholesale distribution services. We understand the importance of consistent supply and work to ensure businesses have access to the products they need.

Whether you're looking for everyday cleaning supplies, hygiene products for your facilities, or office consumables for your workplace, VADORA Supply Solutions is committed to delivering practical business solutions.
